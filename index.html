About Us
Built for Builders

Pat Bigelow Painting specializes in new home construction painting. We've been the go-to painting crew for builders and developers across Dodge County for over two decades — because we understand the pace, the standards, and the pressure of construction timelines.

Builder-first scheduling. We sync with your construction timeline, not the other way around. We show up when the drywall's ready and finish before the next trade walks in.

Consistent crew, consistent quality. Same experienced painters on every job. No revolving door of subcontractors — you know exactly who's on your site.

Volume pricing for developments. Building 10+ homes? We offer competitive per-unit pricing that keeps your margins healthy without sacrificing quality.

Premium products, always. Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore — no shortcuts. Better coverage means fewer coats and happier homeowners down the road.
